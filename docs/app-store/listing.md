## Description
LiveMaker makes Live Photos without a subscription and without uploading
your pictures anywhere.

• Animate a photo: pick any still image, choose an effect — falling snow,
  twinkling starlight, slow zoom, or snow + zoom — and LiveMaker renders
  the animation on-device, frame by frame. Your original photo is never
  redrawn or sent to a server.
• Video to Live Photo: pick a video, choose the cover frame, save it as a
  Live Photo.
• Set it as wallpaper: open the saved Live Photo in Photos → Share →
  Use as Wallpaper for a moving lock screen.

The app is free and supported by a single small banner ad. No account,
no tracking of your photos, no paywall.
