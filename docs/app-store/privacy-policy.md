# LiveMaker Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-12 (draft — set the real date at publication)

LiveMaker ("the app") is developed by LULULAND STUDIO LLC ("we").
This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

## Your photos and videos stay on your device
All conversion happens locally on your iPhone. The app accesses only the
photos and videos you explicitly pick, processes them on the device, and
saves the resulting Live Photo to your photo library. We do not upload,
store, or have access to your photos, videos, or the Live Photos you
create. The app has no account system and no server of ours receives your
content.

## Advertising
The app is free and shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve
and measure ads, Google may collect and use:
- device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, IDFA, only
  if you allow tracking in the system prompt),
- usage data (such as ad views and interactions),
- diagnostics and performance data.
This data is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can decline the tracking
prompt — the app still works fully; ads may be less personalized.

## Data we collect directly
None. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, or
usage analytics through any system of our own.

## Children
The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect
personal data from children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same
URL with a new effective date.

## Contact
Questions: renxuelu@gmail.com
